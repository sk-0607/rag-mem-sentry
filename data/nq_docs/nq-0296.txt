In an interview with Zane Lowe for Beats 1 Radio, The Weeknd stated that he and Daft Punk member Guy-Manuel de Homem-Christo had been mutual friends long before the recording of Starboy. When he visited a recording studio in Paris, France, Daft Punk presented The Weeknd with the draft instrumental for "I Feel It Coming". He claims that he wrote the lyrics in one hour.[1]

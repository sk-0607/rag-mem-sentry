"Habits (Stay High)" charted in various European countries. It spent one week on the Euro Digital Songs, where it peaked at number 19.[82] In Germany, the song debuted at number 79 on the Top 100 Singles chart on 30 May 2014. It eventually peaked at number 14 and spent 53 weeks on the chart.[83] It was certified triple-gold by the Bundesverband Musikindustrie (BVMI), denoting 450,000 sales in the country.[84] In France, the song peaked at number two on the Top Singles Téléchargés chart for the issue dated 6 September 2014.[85] According to Pure Charts, the song sold over 88,600 copies during 2014 in France, becoming the thirteenth best-selling song of that year in that country.[86] It spent a total of 75 non-consecutive weeks on the chart between 2014 and 2016.[85] It also reached number two on the airplay chart and topped the streaming chart of the country.[87][88] In Hungary, the recording peaked at number nine on the sales chart and at number eight on the streaming chart, both published by the Association of Hungarian Record Companies (MAHASZ).[89][90] In Poland, the song debuted at number 11 on the Polish Airplay Chart for the issue dated 1 November 2014.[91] It eventually topped the chart for four consecutive weeks.[92][93][94][95] In Denmark, the song debuted and peaked at number ten on the Track Top 40 chart.[96] It further reached number six on the Airplay Top 20 chart, and received a Platinum certification accorded by IFPI Denmark, denoting 2,600,000 streams in the country.[97][98] "Habits (Stay High)" peaked at number three in both Austria and Switzerland,[99][100] and was certified Gold in both countries by the International Federation of the Phonographic Industry (IFPI).[101][102] In Romania, the song topped the International Airplay Songs Chart on the issue dated 15 December 2014.[103] It reached the top ten on the digital chart of Czech Republic,[104] Luxembourg,[105] Portugal and Slovakia.[106][107] In Spain, it peaked at number 13 on both the sales and airplay singles charts published by the Productores de Música de España (PROMUSICAE).[108][109]

On November 2, 2010, McDonald's began six weeks of nationwide McRib availability at the Legends of the McRib event in New York City, honoring three superfans: Joey Erwin, aka Mr. McRib; Alan Klein, founder of the McRib Locator website; and Adam Winer.[12] The promotion ended December 5, 2010.[13] McDonald's credited it with boosting their November 2010 sales by 4.8%.[14] It was the first national offering of the McRib since 1994.[15]

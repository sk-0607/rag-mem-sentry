Pardes Mein Hai Mera Dil (English: My Heart Lives In A Foreign Land) is a 2016 daily Indian soap opera that premiered on Star Plus on 7 November 2016.[3] Produced by Ekta Kapoor and Shobha Kapoor, the series stars Arjun Bijlani, Drashti Dhami and Vineet Raina. It is inspired by Shah Rukh Khan's 1997 film, Pardes.[4][5] as well as Manju Kapur's 2008 novel The Immigrant.[6] The series is shot in India, as well as in the picturesque cities Vienna and Innsbruck of Austria.[7] The show is dubbed in Malayalam as Akkareyanente Manasam and is currently airing on Asianet Plus.

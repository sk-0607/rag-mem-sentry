By mid-December 1950, the United States was discussing terms for an agreement to end the Korean War.[8] The desired agreement would end the fighting, provide assurances against its resumption, and protect the future security of UNC forces.[9] The United States asked for a military armistice commission of mixed membership that would supervise all agreements.[8] Both sides would need to agree to "cease the introduction into Korea of any reinforcing air, ground or naval units or personnel... and to refrain from increasing the level of war equipment and material existing in Korea."[8] The U.S. wished to create a demilitarized zone that would be roughly 20-mile  -wide  (32Â km).[8] The proposed agreement would also address the issue of prisoners of war which the U.S. believed should be exchanged on a one-for-one basis.[8]

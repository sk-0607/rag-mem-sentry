Gerry Faust was hired to replace Devine in 1981.[210] Prior to Notre Dame, Faust had been one of the more successful high school football coaches in the country.[211] As coach of Moeller High School in Cincinnati he amassed a 174–17–2 record over 19 seasons.[211] Many of his players had gone on to play for Notre Dame; indeed, when he arrived in South Bend, he was reunited with nine of his former players from Moeller.

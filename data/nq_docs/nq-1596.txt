In 2009, Wegmans ranked 29th on the Supermarket News list of the Top 75 North American Food Retailers based on sales volume,[36] up from number 32 on the 2007 Supermarket News list,[3] and 7th among privately held U.S. supermarkets.[citation needed] In 2009 Stores Magazine showed it to be the 74th-largest retailer in the United States with estimated revenues of $4.67 billion[37] As of 2006, it was the 66th largest privately held company, as determined by Forbes.[38] On Forbes's 2005 list, Wegmans ranked 54th.[39]

The Air Force's civilian auxiliary, Civil Air Patrol, wear-tested the ABU in late 2015. In May 2016, the national commander of the Civil Air Patrol issued a memorandum allowing the wear of the ABU effective June 15, 2016.[11][12] However, the uniform is worn with dark blue name tapes (with white lettering) and insignia cloth (similar to those used by the USAF in the 1970s) and black boots in order to distinguish CAP personnel.

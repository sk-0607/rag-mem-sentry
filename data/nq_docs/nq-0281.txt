Two months after the failure of this congress to write a new constitution, Bolívar was declared president-liberator in Colombia's "Organic Decree".[4]:394 He considered this as a temporary measure, as a means to reestablish his authority and save the republic, although it increased dissatisfaction and anger among his political opponents.[4]:408 An assassination attempt on 25 September 1828 failed (in Spanish it is indeed known as the Noche Septembrina), thanks to the help of his lover, Manuela Sáenz.[4]:399–405 Bolívar afterward described Manuela as "Liberatrix of the Liberator".[4]:403 Dissent continued, and uprisings occurred in New Granada, Venezuela, and Ecuador during the next two years.[28]

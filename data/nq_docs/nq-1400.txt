In early 1975,[207] The Jim Halsey Company began Country Music USA, a show at the Landmark that featured a different country music headliner every two to three weeks. The show was usually sold out.[208] Roy Clark and Mel Tillis made their debuts in Country Music USA,[209] as did Freddy Fender.[208] The Oak Ridge Boys made their Las Vegas debut in Country Music USA.[208][209] Leroy Van Dyke performed in the show during 1975, with Fender as his opening act.[210][211] Van Dyke performed again at the Landmark later in the 1970s, with Sons of the Pioneers as his opening act.[211] Other artists who performed in Country Music USA included Barbara Fairchild, Johnny Paycheck and Tommy Overstreet,[212] as well as Jody Miller, Roy Head, and Hank Thompson.[213] Country Music USA ran for two years, until 1977.[213]

The Cosmic Love Tour kicked off at Dublin's Olympia Theatre on 2 May 2010 where the group performed a new song called "Strangeness and Charm"[119] and ended at London's Hammersmith Apollo on 15 May 2010.[120] On 25 June, the group played the Glastonbury Festival 2010, where they performed "Strangeness and Charm" and a cover of Fleetwood Mac's "The Chain" during their ten-song set.[121] Their performance drew one of the biggest crowds of the day and one of the biggest in the Other Stage's history.[121]

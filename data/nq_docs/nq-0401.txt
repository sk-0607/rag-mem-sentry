Buescher returned to the No. 60 in 2015.[2] Cup sponsors Fastenal, Cheez-It, Safety-Kleen, and AdvoCare came on to sponsor several races, along with Bit-O-Honey and Salted Nut Roll manufactured by the Pearson's Candy Company.[91][92] Buescher finished second in the Daytona season-opener behind teammate Ryan Reed.[1][81] He scored his first victory of the season at Iowa in May, on a green-white-checkered finish.[93] He scored his second win later in the month at Dover, after pit-stop strategy and contact with pole sitter and teammate Darrell Wallace Jr. racing for the lead.[94][95] After 24 consecutive weeks as the points leader, Buescher won his first Xfinity Series title and the eighth for Roush, with 11 top fives, 20 top tens, and an average finish of 8.4.[68][96][97]

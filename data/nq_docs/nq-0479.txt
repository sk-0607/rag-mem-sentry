Several VCs have been stolen and, being valuable, have been placed on the Interpol watch-list for stolen items.[93] The VC awarded to Milton Gregg, which was donated to the Royal Canadian Regiment Museum in London, Ontario Canada in 1979, was stolen on Canada Day (1 July 1980), when the museum was overcrowded[94] and has been missing since. A VC awarded in 1917 to Canadian soldier Corporal Filip Konowal[95] was stolen from the same museum in 1973 and was not recovered until 2004.[96]

In mid-2009 Cadbury replaced some of the cocoa butter in their non-UK chocolate products with palm oil. Despite stating this was a response to consumer demand to improve taste and texture, there was no "new improved recipe" claim placed on New Zealand labels. Consumer backlash was significant from environmentalists and chocolate lovers. By August 2009, the company announced that it was reverting to the use of cocoa butter in New Zealand.[32] In addition, they would source cocoa beans through Fair Trade channels.[33] In January 2010 prospective buyer Kraft pledged to honour Cadbury's commitment.[34]

About 5 miles (8.0Â km) west of Soda Springs, "Hudspeth's Cutoff" (est. 1849)[74] took off from the main trail heading almost due west and by-passed Fort Hall. Hudspeth's Cutoff had five mountain ranges to cross and took about the same amount of time as the main route to Fort Hall but many took it thinking it was shorter. Its main advantage was that it did spread out the traffic on busy years and made more grass available.

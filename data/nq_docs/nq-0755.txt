The population of Ireland stood at 4,588,252 in 2011, an increase of 8.2% since 2006.[137] As of 2011[update], Ireland had the highest birth rate in the European Union (16 births per 1,000 of population).[138] In 2014, 36.3% of births were to unmarried women.[139] Annual population growth rates exceeded 2% during the 2002-2006 intercensal period, which was attributed to high rates of natural increase and immigration.[140] This rate declined somewhat during the subsequent 2006-2011 intercensal period, with an average annual percentage change of 1.6%.

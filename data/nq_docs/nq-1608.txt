The wealth of former NDP secretary Ezz was estimated at 18 billion Egyptian pounds;[101] the wealth of former housing minister Ahmed al-Maghraby was estimated at more than 11 billion Egyptian pounds;[101] that of former tourism minister Zuhair Garrana is estimated at 13 billion Egyptian pounds;[101] former minister of trade and industry Rashid Mohamed Rashid is estimated to be worth 12 billion Egyptian pounds,[101] and former interior minister Habib al-Adly was estimated to be worth eight billion Egyptian pounds.[101] The perception among Egyptians was that the only people benefiting from the nation's wealth were businessmen with ties to the National Democratic Party: "Wealth fuels political power and political power buys wealth."[102]

In 2010, journalist Andrew Bolt was sued in the Federal Court over two posts on his Herald Sun blog in 2009. Bolt was found to have contravened the Racial Discrimination Act 1975 (Cth) in 2011 following comments regarded to be representative of a "eugenic" approach to aboriginal identity.[63][64] This prompted the federal government to propose changes to the Racial Discrimination Act but has met with stiff resistance.[65]

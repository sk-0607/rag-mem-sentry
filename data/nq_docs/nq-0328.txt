On July 21, 2009, Armstrong announced that he would return to the Tour de France in 2010.[52] RadioShack was named as the main sponsor for Armstrong's 2010 team, named Team RadioShack.[53] Armstrong made his 2010 season debut at the Tour Down Under where he finished 25th out of the 127 riders who completed the race. He made his European season debut at the 2010 Vuelta a Murcia finishing in seventh place overall. Armstrong was also set to compete in several classics such as the Milan–San Remo, Amstel Gold Race, Liège–Bastogne–Liège, and the Tour of Flanders, but bouts with gastroenteritis forced his withdrawal from three of the four races.[54]

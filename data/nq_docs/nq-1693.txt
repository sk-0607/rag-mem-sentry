The airport is served by various Ridesharing and taxi companies, and is accessible from highways Interstate 880, and US 101 via State Route 87.[96]  There are six parking lots, including Economy Lot 1, Hourly Lots 2, 3, and 5 and Daily Lots 4 and 6. Rental car operations are located at the multi-story CONRAC garage across from Terminal B.[97] A free cellphone waiting area exists across State Route 87 from the airport.[98] Inter-terminal and Economy parking lot busing is provided by the airport at no charge.

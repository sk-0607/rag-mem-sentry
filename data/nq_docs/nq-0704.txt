The U.S. Marine Corps plans to purchase 340 F-35B and 80 F-35C models[335] to replace current inventories of both the F/A-18 Hornet (A, B, C and D-models), and the AV-8B Harrier II, in the fighter and attack roles.[336] The Marines plan to use the F-35B from "unimproved surfaces at austere bases" but with "special, high-temperature concrete designed to handle the heat".[337][338] The USMC declared initial operational capability with about 50 F-35s running interim block 2B software on 31 July 2015.[339] The USAF had considered replacing the A-10 with the F-35B, but will not do so because of the F-35B's inability to generate enough sorties.[340]

The album debuted at No. 7 on the Billboard 200,[158] No. 1 on the Classical Albums chart[159] and No. 22 on the Canadian Albums chart.[160] The ranking made the 12-year-old the second artist who ever "amassed three top 10 albums [on the Billboard 200] at such a young age."[161] The album remained on the Billboard 200 for 13 weeks and appeared on the Billboard Classical Albums chart in 72 weeks.[162] Songs from the Silver Screen was the No. 4 Classical Album of 2013[163] and the No. 36 Classical Album of 2014.[164]

S. pneumoniae played a central role in demonstrating that genetic material consists of DNA. In 1928 Frederick Griffith demonstrated transformation of life turning harmless pneumococcus into a lethal form by co-inoculating the live pneumococci into a mouse along with heat-killed virulent pneumococci.[12] In 1944 Oswald Avery, Colin MacLeod, and Maclyn McCarty demonstrated the transforming factor in Griffith's experiment was not protein, as was widely believed at the time, but DNA.[13] Avery's work marked the birth of the molecular era of genetics.[14]

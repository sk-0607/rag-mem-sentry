In 67 BC, the Han empire gained absolute control over the Turfan Depression after inflicting a significant defeat to the Xiongnu.[159] During the former Xiongnu rule of the Western Regions, the area was under the jurisdiction of the Rizhu King (日逐王) with the office "Commandant in Charge of Slaves".[161] However, in 60 BC, the Rizhu King surrendered to Protector General Zheng Ji.[161] Afterwards (60 BC), the Han imperial court established the Protectorate of the Western Regions.[65][159][162] The Han empire, now in absolute control of the Western Regions, placed it under the jurisdiction of its Protector General.[163][164] As its dominance of the area was established, the Han were effectively controlling the trade and shaping the early history of what would be known as the Silk Road.[65]

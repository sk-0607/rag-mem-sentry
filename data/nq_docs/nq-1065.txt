It tells the story of an Arizona Ranger's duel with an outlaw named Texas Red in the "town of Agua Fria".[3]  The townspeople predict the death of the Ranger, Texas Red having already killed "one and nineteen men", but the Ranger kills Texas Red with the swiftness of the "big iron on his hip." This term refers to the gun the Ranger carries. Texas Red hadn't even "cleared leather" - the Ranger's draw was that swift.

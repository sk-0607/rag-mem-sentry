Wade turned to sports, especially basketball and football, to avoid the temptations of participating in drug and gang-related activities. Wade's mom and dad would often take him to the park to play basketball. He cites one of his older sisters, Tragil, as the individual most responsible for his childhood upbringing and for steering him in the proper direction.[6] As a child growing up in the Chicago area, Wade idolized Chicago Bulls star Michael Jordan,[7][8] and has said he patterns his game after him.

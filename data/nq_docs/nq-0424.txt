As the novel opens, each planet is ruled by a Great House that owes allegiance to the Padishah Emperor Shaddam IV. The Emperor is aware that Duke Leto Atreides of House Atreides has become a potential challenger to his throne as he gains favor with other Great Houses in the Landsraad. The Emperor seeks to have House Atreides destroyed by assigning them control of Arrakis, currently ruled by Baron Vladimir Harkonnen of House Harkonnen. The Atreides and Harkonnen houses have had a generations-long feud, and the Emperor secretly plots with the Baron to attack House Atreides after they take control of Arrakis. While masking his involvement, the Emperor provides the Baron with some of his elite Sardaukar troops to aid the Baron's attack in disguise.

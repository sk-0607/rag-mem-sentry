Makoto Shinkai, director of Your Name, called the film a "fantastic piece of work" and a "polished and grand production" which even he is unable to replicate.[28] It won Best Animation of the Year in the 26th Japan Movie Critics Awards, where director Naoko Yamada also received praise for her work on the film.[29] At 2017 Annecy International Animated Film Festival, the film was selected as one of the nine feature films in competition.[30]

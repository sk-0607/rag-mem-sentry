DJs Meck and Dino created a mashup of the former's 2007 single, "Feels Like Home", with "Like a Prayer" and released it with the name as "Feels Like a Prayer". The song reached the top ten in Belgium (Flanders region) and the Netherlands, while peaking at number 15 in Belgium (Walloon region).[135] In the United States, "Feels Like a Prayer" reached number seven on the Hot Dance Club Songs chart.[135] We Are the Fallen, an American gothic metal band, covered "Like a Prayer" live in 2008. Nick Duerden from Spin complimented the performance and the track for being "so successfully pulverized that one wondered whether it wasn't written specifically to become the world's greatest heavy rock anthem."[136] In June 2017, Leonard released a piano ballad version of the track, with vocals from singer Dana Williams.[6] In the same month, Brazilian singer Luiza Possi covered "Like a Prayer" during the show Domingão do Faustão, in a similar performance to that of Blond Ambition World Tour.[137] She later sang it during the encore of a show in tribute to Michael Jackson, which took place in July.[138]

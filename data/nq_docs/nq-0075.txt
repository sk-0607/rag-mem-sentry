Since 1 January 2009 same-sex couples have had the same access to military retirement pensions and superannuation as opposite-sex couples.[166] Transgender personnel are also permitted to serve in the ADF, and are provided with support when necessary.[167] The ADF has permitted a contingent of gay and lesbian personnel to march together in the Sydney Mardi Gras since 2008, and from 2013 these personnel have been authorised to wear their uniforms during the parade.[168]

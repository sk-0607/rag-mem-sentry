The title never actually appears in the song's lyrics. Lennon later revealed that, like "A Hard Day's Night", it was taken from one of Ringo Starr's malapropisms.[13] In a television interview in early 1964, Starr had uttered the phrase "Tomorrow never knows" when laughing off an incident that took place at the British Embassy in Washington, DC, during which one of the guests had cut off a portion of his hair.[14] The piece was originally titled "Mark I".[8] "The Void" is cited as another working title but according to Mark Lewisohn (and Bob Spitz) this is untrue.
